# Pancreatic Cancer Survival Analysis — R & Python

**Survival analysis on pancreatic cancer clinical trial data using Kaplan-Meier curves and Cox proportional hazards regression.**

MSc Health Data Science project (University of Liverpool, 2024).

---

## What this does

- Applies Kaplan-Meier survival analysis to estimate overall survival across patient cohorts
- Fits Cox proportional hazards regression to identify significant risk factors and quantify their impact (hazard ratios)
- Produces publication-quality survival curves, hazard ratio plots, and statistical summaries
- Analyses how adverse events and treatment variables affect survival outcomes

---

## Key findings

Cox regression identified several risk factors not immediately obvious from raw Kaplan-Meier curves alone — demonstrating the value of multivariate modelling over simple descriptive analysis. Results documented with full statistical outputs including confidence intervals and p-values.

---

- R (survival, survminer, ggplot2)
